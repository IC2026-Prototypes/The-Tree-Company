Tender Submission — Council Parks Maintenance, North Sydney LGA

Submitted to: North Sydney Council · Procurement Division

1. Executive Summary

The Tree Company is pleased to submit this tender for the ongoing maintenance and management of council-owned trees across the North Sydney Local Government Area. With over 15 years of specialised arboricultural experience serving local governments across New South Wales, we are ideally positioned to deliver the high standard of care, safety, and compliance this contract demands.

Our team of ISA-certified arborists, supported by industry-leading equipment and a proven track record with councils including Parramatta City, Hornsby Shire, and Lane Cove, ensures North Sydney Council receives expert, reliable, and professionally documented tree management services.

2. Company Overview

The Tree Company is a fully certified, insured arboricultural firm operating across the greater Sydney and NSW regions. Our services span the complete spectrum of tree management, including:

  • Risk assessment and QTRA/VTA reports
  • Scheduled pruning and canopy management
  • Hazardous tree removal and emergency response
  • Stump grinding and site rehabilitation
  • Development application arborist reports

3. Methodology & Service Delivery

Our service delivery model is built on a proactive inspection schedule, real-time digital reporting, and a dedicated council liaison officer for all North Sydney engagements. Each tree will be assessed against AS 4373-2007 and relevant council tree management policies, with all works documented via our digital field management system.
